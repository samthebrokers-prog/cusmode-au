How it works

Simple for you and your NZ customer.

Send the documents

Commercial invoice, packing list, and the bill of lading or air waybill, before or as the goods ship.

We check everything

NZ tariff, duty, GST and MPI requirements, and we flag any problems early.

We lodge the NZ entry

Customs and biosecurity entries lodged through New Zealand's Trade Single Window.

Cleared & released

Goods released in NZ to your forwarder or carrier for delivery to your customer.
